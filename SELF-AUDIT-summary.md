## What it is

A free, self-serve web tool for trades businesses. They answer a short questionnaire and it shows them — in **hours and dollars** — what their manual / un-systemized processes are costing them every year, then routes them to a call with us. It's the front door of the whole business: education → audit → build.

## Why it works (the logic)

Three things, none of which need a "perfect blueprint":
1. **The mirror** — most tradies have never seen the full list of systems a business *could* run. Showing it creates the "oh shit" moment.
2. **The number** — they feel the pain but have never measured it. We put a defensible figure on invisible waste.
3. **The possibility** — what AI/systems can specifically do for them (the part competitors aren't doing).

It also captures the lead + their #1 priority, so we ring them warm, already knowing the opener.

## Two versions

- **FULL audit** — comprehensive ~76-system check vs a fully-systemized business. The deep mirror. → our **guided / in-person** tool.
- **LEAN audit** — "What's AI worth to you?", ~14 quick questions (what's eating your time → what AI does → what it's worth). → the **public front door** (lower friction, higher completion).

## The flow (end to end)

1. **Inputs:** their hourly value, team size, # customers in their job system, average job value, and *"do you do the office work or have staff?"*
2. **They tick** what they've got (full) / what still eats their time (lean)
3. **Live total** builds as they go
4. **Email gate** (name / email / phone / business) → captures the lead
5. **Result:** wasted **time ($)** and recoverable **revenue ($)**, shown as two separate believable numbers
6. **"If you could take ONE thing off your plate first — which?"** → captures their #1 priority
7. **Auto-emails them** a branded proposal (their number + what AI could do + "Systems will set you free" + book-a-call)
8. **Alerts us** with their name, phone and their #1 priority — so the call opens on exactly what they want

## How the numbers work (methodology) 👉 *this is where I most want your gut-check*

- **Time** = `frequency × minutes per task` (bottom-up, shows the working — e.g. "8 quotes/wk × 25 min = 3.3 hrs"). Anchored to industry data: a *solo* tradie loses ~7–8 hrs/wk to admin alone; quoting ~2–3 hrs/wk; invoicing + chasing ~4 hrs/wk; job-management software saves ~6 hrs/wk.
- **Scaling** — tasks scale on different drivers: *per-business* (fixed, e.g. bank rec), *per-person* (e.g. stock take, timesheets), *per-job* (e.g. quoting, invoicing). The tool flexes with their team size + volume.
- **Value (who does it)** = two rates: **owner / on-the-tools (~$120)** vs **admin / staff (~$35)**, set by the "do you have office staff?" question. Captures that an owner doing $35 admin is really burning $120 time.
- **Revenue** = database reactivation (customers × ~1%/mo × avg job) + dead-quote revival.
- **Proposal** = ~**10% of total annual value** — the anchor that justifies our fee (≈ a retainer), not a number from thin air.
