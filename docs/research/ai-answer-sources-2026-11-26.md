# Sources AI answers draw on

Summary: for NZ tradie automation questions, the sites that keep showing up are one prolific NZ blog (Enterprise DNA), agency directories (DesignRush, Clutch, Sortlist, The Manifest), software marketplaces (Simpro, Fergus, Xero), software review sites (Capterra and GetApp NZ) and NZ tech media (IT Brief). Flowstate was not seen on any of them.

## Limits

- This comes from ordinary web search results (standard mode, one search per question or topic, 2026-11-26), not from the AI engines' own citations. A site that ranks in search is likely, not certain, to be used by an AI engine.
- The only engine data used is `docs/baseline/results/gemini.csv`. It records company names the answers mentioned, not the URLs cited, so it shows who is named, not which pages were read.
- The search tool is US-only, so NZ rankings may differ. Reddit and forum threads did not appear in any result; that is a gap, not proof they are unused.
- Most pages found are vendor marketing. "How Flowstate could appear" is a suggestion only. Nothing was signed up for, submitted or contacted. Cost is "unknown" where not confirmed.

## Competitors seen and where

- Ez-AI: DesignRush, Sortlist, The Manifest, Clutch. Strongest directory footprint.
- Muscle+Brain: DesignRush, GoodFirms.
- Leadica: Fergus marketplace (the only automation agency seen on a software vendor's partner page).
- e2e Ltd: Simpro case study and partner pages.
- Sophiie: own news pages, Startup Daily, Business News Australia, Australian government AI directory, Dealroom, Tradiespace partnership.
- Waboom, Callmate, LANA, CallCrewAI: own sites, Simpro marketplace (LANA, CallCrewAI), Capterra and GetApp NZ.
- Automate AI, Stride AI, Wisely, Hyper Digital, TradieCall, Talkify, Torque AI, Fylo, H3llo, FirstVoice: no listings found in these searches. Worth a separate look before drawing conclusions.

## Top 10 to act on

1. Enterprise DNA (enterprisedna.co): it ranks for almost every question, so ask for a mention or build competing NZ tradie guides with real local detail.
2. DesignRush profile: it feeds "AI automation agency Auckland" results (Q10) and Ez-AI is there, and a profile is cheap.
3. Clutch NZ AI companies page: ranks for Auckland AI agency searches and rewards client reviews.
4. Capterra NZ and GetApp NZ: they hold the AI receptionist and electrical contractor categories behind Q4, Q7 and Q12.
5. Simpro marketplace and partner pages: the only route into Q6 answers; check partner requirements first because the effort is high.
6. Fergus marketplace: Leadica shows an automation agency can be listed for Q5; find out how.
7. IT Brief NZ and ChannelLife NZ: a news mention on NZ trades AI would be a strong, free third-party signal.
8. LinkedIn company page and founder posts: a LinkedIn post already ranks repeatedly, and this is the lowest effort win.
9. Sortlist, The Manifest and GoodFirms: more agency profiles that repeat the same Q10 names, to do after the first three.
10. Master Electricians NZ: its "AI on the Tools" course and member benefits fit the electrician focus; start with a conversation, not a listing.
