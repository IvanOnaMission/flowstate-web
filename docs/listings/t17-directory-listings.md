**Name:** Flow State

**Tagline:**
AI automation for NZ trade businesses, built on Fergus and the software you already use.

**About Flow State:**
Flow State builds AI helpers for New Zealand trade businesses, mainly electricians, plus plumbers and HVAC. We use the Fergus API to take admin off the owner: quotes started from a voice note on your phone, job updates, scheduling, and your job and financial info pulled into one place.

Fergus does the most when it's connected to everything else. We link it to Xero, your email, your calendar and a company brain that holds your prices, rules and processes, so the AI sees the whole business instead of one piece of it.

The AI drafts and you approve. Nothing goes to a customer without your OK, and anything it can't price is marked CHECK instead of guessed. Everything runs on your own accounts, so you own the system.

Jordan's own Auckland electrical business has quotes coming back almost fully built and added 150 reviews in three months.

We start with a 30-minute call to work out what's costing your business the most. The foundation (accounts in your name, your software connected, the company brain) is set up in 2 to 3 days, then we add helpers one at a time.

Flow State is an independent business. Our services are provided by Flow State, not by Fergus.

**Services:**

- **Quotes from a voice note.** Talk through the job on your phone and the AI builds a draft quote in Fergus from your price list. You check it and send it.
- **Job updates and scheduling.** Keep jobs and the schedule up to date in Fergus without typing it all in after a long day.
- **Invoices and payments through Xero.** The Fergus API doesn't create invoices or record payments, so we run that side through Xero, drafted for you to approve.
- **Lead and quote follow-up.** New enquiries get a reply drafted within minutes, and unanswered quotes get followed up at 3 and 7 days. It stops as soon as the customer replies.
- **Google review requests.** When a job is paid, a review request is drafted to go out from your own phone or email. It never asks for 5 stars and never asks only the happy customers.
- **A company brain.** Your prices, rules and processes in one place, so every helper knows how your business runs.

**Industry Expertise:** Electrical, Plumbing, HVAC

**Support Regions:** NZ

**Partner Website / Enquire Now:** https://flowstate.net.nz/contact
