A few things you might be wondering.

Is the diagnostic free?

The introductory call is free. The diagnostic is paid work: we agree the scope and fee before starting. You leave with a review of your workflows and a prioritised roadmap.

What is an AI brain?

It’s your company data and your team’s knowledge, organised into a folder system. That knowledge gives AI context and forms the foundation for the systems we build. You review what goes into it and decide who has access.

Is the AI brain included in the diagnostic?

The diagnostic finds the best use case to start with. We then agree the work to capture your business knowledge, build the systems and train your team.

How long does the setup take?

That depends on the scope, your tools and the information we need. We agree a delivery plan after the diagnostic, before the build starts.

Will it work with my software?

We check what your tools can connect to during the diagnostic. The plan reflects the access your software supports and any requirements we need to address.

Will AI decide my prices or send work out for me?

You keep control of pricing and technical decisions. AI can help prepare drafts in the workflows we agree. You check the details and approve important actions before they go out.

Do I need to be good with AI?

You don’t need to work out the setup yourself. We help you use it on real tasks and check the results. For the introductory call, there’s nothing to prepare.

What happens after the setup?

We review how it’s being used and discuss the next priorities. Further builds, training and ongoing support are scoped and agreed with you.
