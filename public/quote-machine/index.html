How the 30 days work.

The Quote Machine is built for you over 30 days, around your prices, your software and the way you already quote.

  1. Week 1Setup and a first win.

    We set your team up, connect your tools and record how long your quotes take now. We also draft follow-ups for every quote from the last 90 days that wasn’t accepted, ready for you to send.

  2. Weeks 1 to 3Mapping and building.

    We map how you quote and capture your pricing rules: where prices come from, what labour each price includes, margins, GST and rounding. Then we build quote drafting from site notes, photos or plans, and test it against jobs you’ve already quoted.

  3. Week 4Training and handover.

    The people who quote are trained on it, and it’s handed over working on your real jobs.

What stays with you.

You approve every quote.

Nothing goes to a customer until you’ve checked it and sent it.

Missing rates are asked for, not guessed.

It’s tested before it goes live.

Drafts are checked against quotes you’ve already approved before your team relies on them.

Electrical and plumbing businesses where quoting takes up the owner’s or estimator’s time and quotes go out later than they should.

Questions you might have.

My pricing is messy. Will it still work?

Yes. Tidying your existing prices, kit sets and labour rules and loading them in is part of the build. If you don’t have a pricing system at all, we’ll talk on the call about building one first.

What if it gets a price wrong?

Before it goes live, its drafts are checked against jobs you’ve already quoted and approved. After that, nothing goes to a customer until you approve it, and a missing rate comes back to you as a question instead of a guessed price.

I don’t have time to set this up.

It’s built for you over the 30 days. What we need from you is your price list, five past quotes you’ve approved, and some time in a few setup sessions.

Will it work with my software?

It connects to Fergus, Tradify, Xero or Groundplan where the software allows it, and works from an export where it doesn’t. We check your setup on the call, before you commit to anything.

How long before it pays off?

The first piece of work in week one is drafting follow-ups for every unaccepted quote from the last 90 days, so you have something to send before the build is finished.

My team doesn’t use AI. Will they use this?

Everyone is set up and taught the basics in the first week, and the people who quote are trained on the system before it’s handed over.

How much quoting time could I save?

It depends on how you quote now and which parts we can take off your hands. We record how long your quotes take in week one and compare that with the system working on your real jobs. What’s guaranteed is the handover within 30 days.

What does the guarantee need from me?

Your price list and five past approved quotes in week one, your attendance at the setup sessions, and using the drafts for every standard quote.

What does it cost?

It depends on how many people quote and where your prices live now. Those are the two things the call works out, and I’ll give you a price once I know them.
