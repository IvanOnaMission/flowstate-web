Your information

Privacy notice

When you fill in the diagnostic enquiry form, Flowstate collects your name, phone number and email address, your answer about whether you own a business, and any other details you choose to share, so Ivan can contact you about the diagnostic and AI partnership and prepare for your call.

If you requested the quoting workbook, Flowstate collected your name, email address and phone number to deliver it, send practical follow-up emails and let Ivan call you about your quoting and whether Flowstate can help.

Where your details go

The website is hosted by Netlify. Website enquiry form details are stored with Netlify Forms, Twenty (our current customer records; older records may remain in Notion) and Supabase (our marketing database), and Ivan gets a Telegram message with them so he can call you. If you enquire using a Facebook or Instagram instant form, Meta collects the contact details you submit and shares them with Flowstate for the same purpose. Audit enquiries may also go to Kit for a booking reminder. Workbook requests went to Kit for email delivery and follow-up. If you book a call, Calendly handles your booking details. These providers may process information outside New Zealand.

After booking your call, you can complete a short business quiz about your office staff, business challenges, goals, AI experience, timing and investment. Answers, including anything you type under “Other”, are stored with Netlify Forms alongside your booking reference and contact details and copied to your customer record where the booking can be matched. They help Ivan prepare for your call. Quiz answers are not sent to advertising platforms. Your booking stays confirmed if you choose not to complete the quiz.

If you enquire through a Facebook or Instagram instant form, any optional answers you submit about your business and sales process are copied to your customer record so Ivan can prepare for your audit. We receive the information after you submit the form; we do not receive abandoned or unsubmitted answers. You can skip the optional questions and still enquire. Your call is booked separately through Calendly.

If you choose to continue an audit enquiry in Messenger, we use the email you confirm there to match the chat to your enquiry. Each answer you send is saved to your customer record as you go, even if you stop before finishing the questions. Meta handles the Messenger conversation; Flowstate stores the audit replies with Netlify and Twenty and sends Ivan a summary through Telegram, including your contact details and the answers you sent. The questions are optional and you can book a call without completing them.

Your choices

The forms require your name, email and phone number. You can choose not to submit them. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in an email, or ask Ivan to stop calling or emailing you.

Campaign information

We may record which campaign or page led to your request so we can understand which route is useful. We do not put your contact details in advertising tracking links. We use the Meta pixel on these campaign pages to measure visits, form submissions, workbook signups and confirmed call bookings from our Facebook and Instagram ads. Meta may use cookies and browser or device information to match activity with its services. We do not send the name, email, phone number or questionnaire answers you enter in these forms to the pixel.

Questions or corrections

To ask about your information, request access or a correction, or ask us to remove it where possible, email ivan@flowstate.net.nz.
