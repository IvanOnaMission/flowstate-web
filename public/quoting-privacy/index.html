Your information

Quoting workbook privacy notice

When you request the workbook, Flowstate collects your name, email address and phone number to deliver it, send practical follow-up emails and let Ivan call you about your quoting and whether Flowstate can help.

Where your details go

The website is hosted by Netlify. Your workbook request goes to Kit for email delivery and follow-up. If you book a call, Calendly handles your booking details. These providers may process information outside New Zealand.

Your choices

The form requires your name, email and phone number. You can choose not to submit it. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in an email, or ask Ivan to stop calling or emailing you.

Campaign information

We may record which campaign or page led to your request so we can understand which route is useful. We do not put your contact details in advertising tracking links. We use the Meta pixel on these campaign pages to measure visits, workbook signups and confirmed call bookings from our Facebook and Instagram ads. Meta may use cookies and browser or device information to match activity with its services. We do not send the name, email or phone number you enter in this form to the pixel.

Questions or corrections

To ask about your information, request access or a correction, or ask us to remove it where possible, email ivan@flowstate.net.nz.
