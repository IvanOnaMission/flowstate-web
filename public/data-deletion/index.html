Data deletion requests

To request deletion of information held by Flow State, including information received through the Flow State Ads Meta app, a Facebook or Instagram lead form, or our optional Messenger audit chat, email ivan@flowstate.net.nz with the subject “Data deletion request”.

What to include

Tell us the email address you used for your enquiry, whether you used a website form, a Meta instant form or Messenger, and roughly when you enquired. Please do not send passwords or identity documents. We may ask you to confirm control of the enquiry email or conversation so that we can identify your information safely.

What happens next

Ivan handles requests manually. He will locate the matching enquiry and explain what can be removed, any information that needs to be retained, and the outcome of your request. Sending the email does not delete information automatically.

For an audit enquiry, relevant copies may include customer records and notes in Twenty, lead and chat records and queued events stored with Netlify, Telegram notification summaries, marketing records in Supabase, and any Kit booking-reminder subscription. If you booked a call, tell us so the related Calendly booking can also be considered.

Flow State can address copies it controls. Meta may retain the information in your Facebook or Messenger account under its own policies. Deleting a conversation in Messenger does not by itself remove Flow State’s saved enquiry. You can also ask Ivan to stop contacting you or unsubscribe using a marketing email’s unsubscribe link.
