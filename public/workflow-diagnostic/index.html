A few things you might be wondering.

Is the first call free?

Yes. It's 30 minutes of your time to start. If it's a fit, Flow State builds a custom implementation plan, and you and Flow State agree the scope and rate before any build starts.

When is the ongoing AI partner rate agreed?

Before any build starts. Once the priorities and requirements are clear, you and Flow State agree the scope and rate for the ongoing AI partnership, including implementation and support.

Will it work with my software?

Flow State checks your existing tools and the access they support. Any implementation depends on those requirements and the scope you and Flow State agree.

Will AI decide my prices or send quotes for me?

You keep control of pricing and technical decisions. An agreed quoting workflow can help prepare drafts from information you supply. You check the scope, quantities, prices and wording, and approve the quote before it goes out.

What do I need to prepare?

Just 30 minutes to start. Once the build starts, Flow State needs things like your price list, some past quotes and invoices, and access to your job software and Xero, which you log into yourself.

What happens after the call?

If it's a fit, you and Flow State build a custom implementation plan together and agree the scope, rate and delivery plan for the ongoing AI partnership before implementation starts.
