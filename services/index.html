The process
01Audit

We learn the business inside out

A deep-dive call. How jobs flow from quote to invoice. What slows you down. What software you use. What you'd fix first if you could. The more we understand, the sharper the plan.

02Plan

You get it back, written down

A walkthrough of what we found, where the biggest wins are, and what we'd build first — with the time and the money attached to each one. You pick what we start with. One clear plan.

03Build

Foundations first, then the automation

Think of it like onboarding a new team member. First everything the AI needs to know about your business, your jobs, your customers, the way you work. Once that's solid, the automations go on top and plug into your existing tools.
