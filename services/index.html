How it works

The process

Audit → Plan → Build.

Fixed scope
01

Audit

A deep-dive call where we learn your business inside out. How jobs flow from quote to invoice. What slows you down. What software you use. What you'd fix first if you could. The more we understand, the sharper the plan.

02

Plan

We come back with a walkthrough of what we found, where the biggest wins are, and what we'd build first. You pick what we start with. One clear plan.

03

Build

Think of it like onboarding a new team member. First we build the foundations — everything the AI needs to know about your business, your jobs, your customers, the way you work. Once that's solid, we build the automations on top and plug them into your existing tools.
